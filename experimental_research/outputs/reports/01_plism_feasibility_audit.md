## 3. Biological Independence

**Status: Not verified.**

Scanner-specific slide identifiers cannot be treated as independent biological specimens without additional provenance information.

## 4. Coordinate Validation

The tile identifier coordinates agree with the previously generated manifests.

However, this does not independently validate their physical spatial interpretation.

## 5. Dataset Suitability

PLISM is suitable for paired scanner-variation and graph-construction stability experiments.

Independent biological generalization claims require verified specimen identities.

## 6. Required Next Actions

4. Finalize grouped sampling and held-out evaluation.
5. Keep the Phase 1 region separate from final testing.
